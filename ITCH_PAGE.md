## Title
Ember Hollow

## Short description (tagline)
Every wave the cave crumbles faster. Grab gems, bank them quick, and don't get flattened.

## Description

**Every wave the cave crumbles faster.** Grab the gems glowing on the cave floor and race them back to the bank in the wall before the ceiling comes down on your head.

Ember Hollow is a one-thumb arcade game about speed:

- **Speed is your score.** Every gem starts a countdown the moment it appears. Bank it fast for a Lightning bonus of up to +30 points; dawdle and the bonus drains away.
- **Speed is the danger.** Each wave makes the rocks fall faster, drop more often and give less warning. The caveman and the music speed up with them.
- **Slow time is your relief.** Grab a rare blue gem to slow every falling rock for 4 seconds while you keep running at full speed.
- **One hit and you're flattened.** Watch the shadows on the floor; they show where a rock is about to land.

### How to play
- Hold the left side of the screen to run left, the right side to run right. Let go to stop.
- Run over a gem to pick it up, then carry it to the stone bank on the left wall.
- Dodge the falling rocks. One hit ends the run.
- Keyboard: Left/Right arrows or A/D. P or Esc pauses.

### Between runs
- Banked gems carry over. Spend them at the Trader on Quick Feet, Long Arms and Keen Ears.
- Your best score and best wave are saved on your device.

### Theme: Speed
- Speed is rewarded (the speed bonus for every gem), it escalates (faster rocks, runner and music every wave), and it can be relieved (slow-time gems).
